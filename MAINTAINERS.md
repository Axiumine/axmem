organization, and, from the first release, the PyPI project `axmem`).

| Role | Who | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and lead maintainer | Giovanni Manzoni ([@giovannimanzoni](https://github.com/giovannimanzoni)) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository, owner of the PyPI project |

lead maintainer; the release process is in [docs/release.md](docs/release.md). The roles and
how decisions are made are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
