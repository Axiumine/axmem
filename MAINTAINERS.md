# Maintainers

The project has one maintainer. This page lists the maintainer and the other accounts that have access to the sensitive
resources of the project: the GitHub repository and organization, and the PyPI project `axmem`.

| Role | Who | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and lead maintainer | Giovanni Manzoni ([@giovannimanzoni](https://github.com/giovannimanzoni)) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository, only owner of the PyPI project, only required reviewer of the release environments `pypi` and `testpypi` |

## Other accounts with access

These accounts have access but are not maintainers: they have no role in decisions, and none in releases is documented.

| Account | Access (read from the GitHub API on 2026-11-01) | Maintainer |
|---|---|---|
| [@vivi7](https://github.com/vivi7) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository. No access to the PyPI project, not a reviewer of the release environments. | No |

The organization requires two-factor authentication. There is no successor and no co-maintainer: the bus factor is 1, and
nobody is named to take over if the lead maintainer is unavailable. What that means is in
[If the lead is unavailable](GOVERNANCE.md#if-the-lead-is-unavailable). The release process is in
[docs/release.md](docs/release.md). The roles and how decisions are made are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). To report a
vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
