# Maintainers

The people who have access to the sensitive resources of the project: the GitHub repository and
organization, and the PyPI project `axmem`.

| Role | Who | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and lead maintainer | Giovanni Manzoni ([@giovannimanzoni](https://github.com/giovannimanzoni)) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository, owner of the PyPI project, required reviewer of the release environments |
| Co-owner and successor | [@vivi7](https://github.com/vivi7) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository |

Both accounts use two-factor authentication, which the organization requires. The lead maintainer does the day-to-day
work. The co-owner does not yet have access to the PyPI project or to the approval of releases; what that means, and what is
still to do, is in [If the lead is unavailable](GOVERNANCE.md#if-the-lead-is-unavailable). The release process is in
[docs/release.md](docs/release.md). The roles and how decisions are made are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). To report a
vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
